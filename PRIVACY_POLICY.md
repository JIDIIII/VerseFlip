# VerseFlip Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 3, 2026

VerseFlip is a Bible verse memorization app designed to help users save and review Scripture using flashcards.

VerseFlip does not require users to create an account.

VerseFlip does not collect, sell, or share personal information.

The app may store the following information locally on the user’s device:
- Nickname
- Saved Bible verse cards
- Deck names
- Review progress

This information stays on the user’s device and is not sent to the developer.

If the user uses online Bible version features such as ESV or NIV, the app may send the requested Bible reference to the Bible API provider only to retrieve the selected verse text. VerseFlip does not send the user’s name, nickname, or saved deck data to those providers.
